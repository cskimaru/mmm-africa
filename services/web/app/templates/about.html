About us

Medical Missionary Movement, African Chapter

The continental home of the Medical Missionary Movement in Africa, and part of MMM International.

A continental chapter of a global movement

The Medical Missionary Movement is built on the conviction that Christ's healing ministry is best expressed through practical medical service joined with the Gospel message. The African Chapter carries that mission across the continent.

It links national chapters, such as the MMM Kenya Chapter (MMMK), so they can share training, plan joint missions and meet each year at the African Chapter Annual Convention.

Our mission

To unite and equip Christian health workers across Africa to combine medical service with Gospel ministry.

Our vision

An MMM chapter in every African country, and compassionate, Christ-centred care within reach of every community.

How we are organised

One body, separate stewardship

MMM follows the pattern of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. The levels share one mission and work together on shared activities, but each keeps its own leadership and its own finances.
